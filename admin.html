Discovery Interview

Gather enough understanding of the problem — not to design the solution. Focus on outcomes and friction points.

1Outcome — What are you trying to achieve?
The end state, not the solution. What does success look like when this exists?
2Users — Who will use this?
Roles, teams, volume of users and how often they would use it.
3Current Process — How is it done today?
Walk through every step. Where is the friction? What takes the most time?
4Future Process — What would success look like?
Describe the ideal future state in concrete terms. What would change day-to-day?
5Data — What information is required?
What data does the solution need? Where does it come from? Who owns it?
6Constraints — Anything we must do or avoid?
Regulatory, technical, organisational. What are the non-negotiables?
7Value — How would we know this worked?
Agree a measurable outcome upfront. Time saved, accuracy improved, cost reduced — be specific.
